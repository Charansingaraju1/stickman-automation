# CONTENT STRATEGY (90-Day Organic US Growth Plan)

## Channel Positioning
- **Audience:** United States (`en-US`, casual plain American English).
- **Duration Target:** Dynamic **15–30 seconds** adjusted by observed `averageViewPercentage` and retention curve data.
- **Topic Pillars (10):** Scientific facts, impossible hypotheticals, what-if experiments, human body mysteries, space & physics, survival science, unexpected discoveries, everyday objects explained, psychology, surprising history.

## 5-Beat Story Architecture
1. `0–2s`: Frame-0 visual danger/question hook (no greetings, no subscribe beg).
2. `2–7s`: Familiar setup & scenario stakes.
3. `7–15s`: Escalate mechanism & clear scientific visual cutaway.
4. `15–25s`: Surprising consequence / counter-intuitive reveal.
5. `Final 1–2s`: Natural resolution or seamless cut into Frame 0.

## 90-Day Execution Windows (No Fake Engagement)
- **Days 1–14:** Baseline data collection after analytics OAuth upgrade; test pure B&W vector stickman vs hybrid visual cuts.
- **Days 15–30:** Analyze 24h/48h/7d retention & `engagedViews`; eliminate slow hooks and weak sub-categories.
- **Days 31–60:** Iterate single variables per experiment (`duration_band`, `hook_style`, `US posting hour`) while keeping weekly 16:9 compilations for watch hours.
- **Days 61–90:** Scale winning science pillars cleanly toward YPP milestones (track subscribers, 90d Shorts views, 365d watch hours — zero monetization guarantees).
